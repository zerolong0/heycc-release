# Third-Party Notices

[English](#english) | [简体中文](#中文)

## English

The initial public scaffold does not bundle third-party plugin source code, Bridge binaries, or upstream projects with unconfirmed licenses.

Every future external plugin or component must provide when submitted:

- the upstream repository and a fixed Tag/SHA;
- the original license and distribution status;
- a description of HeyCC changes;
- maintainer and security contact information;
- a statement that the upstream author does not endorse HeyCC, where applicable.

## 中文

初始公开骨架不捆绑任何第三方插件源代码、Bridge 二进制或未经确认许可证的上游项目。

未来每个外部插件或组件必须在提交时提供：

- 上游仓库和固定 Tag/SHA；
- 原始许可证与分发许可状态；
- HeyCC 的修改说明；
- 维护者和安全联系入口；
- 不代表上游作者为 HeyCC 背书的声明（如适用）。
